Independent project · Alan Tom · September 2026

Making a public budget easier to inspect.

A civic-data prototype connecting the City’s budget documents to a searchable interface. Built with AI assistance; not affiliated with or endorsed by the City of Syracuse.

The problem and approach

A resident should be able to move from a question to an account’s history and then to the source. The adopted PDF and Auditor workbook answer different questions. This project keeps formal adopted totals separate from workbook proposals and calculated amendment adjustments.

What the review changed

  • Removed subtotals that had been mistaken for individual accounts.
  • Included unnumbered debt and transfer lines that had been omitted.
  • Added checks against exported records, complete result browsing, shareable filtered views, and clearer source labels.

How to try it in two minutes

Open Explore lines, search “overtime,” and open Police Field Services – Sworn. Compare FY26 adopted, FY26 estimate, and FY27 proposal; then follow the workbook sheet and row reference. The different bases matter as much as the amounts.

Evidence and limits

Eight regression tests cover parser and validation failures. Water, Sewer and Sidewalk revenue and expense detail match the transcribed formal totals, as does General Fund revenue. Matching totals does not certify every account as adopted.

The General Fund’s $3 expense residual traces to the Public Works stated total versus its detail. Crouse-Marshall’s $18,000 difference comes from other operating revenue outside the assessment. Downtown has both scope differences and conflicting workbook/PDF values; these remain visible.
