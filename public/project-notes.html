Making the Syracuse budget easy to check.

A plain-language guide to the City's adopted budget for July 2026 to June 2027. It is not a City website and is not endorsed by the City.

What it does

It shows where the money comes from, where it goes and what changed. You can search any of 916 budget lines, see its history back to 2021, and see which official document each number comes from.

Where the numbers come from

Two City documents: the adopted budget book (the official figures) and the City Auditor's budget spreadsheet (each line's history and the proposed budget). The spreadsheet is not official, so the site checks it against the book.

  • The totals for all six City funds match the budget book.
  • 151 lines match the budget book exactly. The site marks them "FY27 adopted".
  • Every other line is shown as proposed, with any final changes the book lists. The site never calls a line adopted unless the book prints that amount.

Checking the budget books

A program reads the tables in the budget book PDF, adds each one up, and compares the result with the total the book prints. It has been run on the last five budget books.

Every remaining difference was traced. All of them are in the books themselves: most are $1 to $3 of rounding, and twelve are real mistakes, such as a minus sign left off or a line left out of a table but still counted in its total. They are listed on the site under "Mistakes we found in the City's budget books".

Figures the PDF prints in a broken font are left out and marked unreadable. They are never guessed.

Try it in two minutes

  • Open Explore lines, choose Money in, and search "Sales Tax". The line shows that it matches the budget book, and on which page.
  • Search "overtime" under Money out. That line is shown as "FY27 proposed", because the book doesn't confirm it.
  • Scroll to About this project to see how far the numbers can be trusted, and the mistakes found in the books.

Limits

  • This is not an audit.
  • Lines the budget book doesn't confirm are proposals, not adopted amounts.
  • The final amendments and the summary text are copied by hand from the budget book.
  • The site has not yet been tested with residents.
