The formal levy should not be substituted for all program operations.

The explorer preserves workbook detail as proposed data and uses the PDF's formal adopted totals in Overview. It does not silently replace conflicting workbook account figures or claim they are adopted.

## What the checks establish

Checks establish internal arithmetic and traceability, not an independent audit of every source account. Matching totals alone does not verify every account's adopted status.
